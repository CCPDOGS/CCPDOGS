Command Control Protection Dogs (CCPD) specializes in breeding, developing, and training elite family protection dogs and executive protection dogs. Our focus is producing exceptional German Shepherds and Belgian Malinois with the genetic foundation, environmental stability, intelligence, and training required for real-world protection.

What Separates CCPD

We believe a truly exceptional protection dog starts with genetics. Our development philosophy is built around three essential elements:

1. Genetics: Strong genetic nerve, temperament, confidence, and natural working ability.
2. Environment: Early exposure, socialization, and development in real-world environments.
3. Training: Structured obedience, environmental conditioning, and advanced protection training.

We begin developing our puppies at five weeks of age. Our dogs are raised in home environments rather than traditional kennel environments, allowing us to develop confident, socially stable dogs from an early age.

Our Training Philosophy

A protection dog must be more than a dog that can bite on command. It must be socially stable, environmentally confident, obedient, and capable of making sound decisions under pressure.

Our objective is to develop dogs that can integrate into everyday family life while maintaining the ability to perform their protection responsibilities when required.

We work what we breed, and breed what we work.
